Water heaters

Tank or tankless? Here's the honest version.

Both are good choices. It comes down to your budget, your space, and how much hot water your family uses.

Tank and tankless water heaters compared
What mattersTankTankless
Upfront costLowerHigher
Typical life here8 to 12 years15 to 20 years
Hot water supplyRuns out when the tank is emptyEndless, one or two showers at a time
SpaceNeeds a closet or garage cornerMounts on a wall
Best forTight budgets and quick swapsBigger families and long-term owners

Valley water is on the hard side. Minerals settle in tanks and coat tankless heat exchangers, so we flush both at every Comfort Club visit.

Repiping

Older home? Newer pipes.

Many homes near downtown Tracy still have galvanized steel pipes from the 1950s and 60s. They rust from the inside, which means low pressure, rusty water, and pinhole leaks.

A repipe fixes all three at once. We map the job, protect your floors, keep water on every night, and patch every access hole before we leave.
