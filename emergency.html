What counts as an emergency

Call Right Away for These.

If you are not sure, call anyway. We would rather tell you it can wait than have you guess.

  • Water spraying or pouring from a pipe, fitting, or water heater
  • A sewer backup into a tub, shower, or floor drain
  • No heat when it is below 40 degrees outside, especially with babies, seniors, or anyone sick at home
  • No AC during a heat wave with someone vulnerable at home
  • A burning or electrical smell from your furnace or AC
  • Your carbon monoxide alarm is going off
  • Water leaking through a ceiling or into a light fixture
  • No water at all in the house

While you wait

What to Do Before We Get There.

These steps keep a bad night from getting worse. We will walk you through them on the phone too.

Water Leak or Burst Pipe

Shut off the water at the main valve. It is usually near the front hose bib or in the garage. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Then open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the pressure.

Leaking Water Heater

Turn off the cold water valve on top of the heater. For a gas heater, turn the gas knob to off or pilot. For an electric heater, switch off its breaker.

Burning Smell from the Furnace or AC

Turn the system off at the thermostat, then switch off its breaker in the panel. Do not turn it back on until a tech checks it.

Gas Smell or a CO Alarm

Leave the house right away with everyone and your pets. Do not flip switches. From outside, call 911 and the gas company, then call us.

Sewer Backup

Stop using water anywhere in the house, including toilets, showers, the dishwasher, and the washer, until we clear the line.

No Heat or No AC

Check that the thermostat is set right and has fresh batteries, and that the breaker has not tripped. Then call us, and close blinds or add blankets while you wait.

Know your valves

Find Your Shutoff Today.

The best time to find your main water shutoff is before you need it.

Walk out to the front of the house and look near the hose bib, or check the garage wall where the water line comes in. If the valve is stuck or rusted, we can replace it during any visit so it works when it counts.
