FAQ

Questions Foothill Homeowners Ask.

Call (559) 392-0743 and tell us what it's doing: no heat at all, cold air, a burner that lights and shuts off, or an error light. We'll tell you straight when we can get there.

The usual causes are a clogged filter, a frozen indoor coil, a dirty outdoor coil, or low refrigerant. Check the filter and make sure the outdoor unit is clear of weeds and leaves. If it still blows warm, shut it off so it doesn't freeze up, and call us.

A dusty smell for the first few minutes of the season is normal: dust on the burners or heat strips is burning off. If it lasts, smells electrical, or smells like gas, turn it off. If you smell gas, get everyone out and call your gas company or 911 from outside.

Check it every month and change it when it looks gray, usually every 1 to 3 months. With pets, smoke season, or dusty summers, change it more often. A clogged filter is one of the most common reasons a system stops heating or cooling well.

A good rule of thumb: if the system is past 15 years old and the repair costs a big share of a new one, replacement is worth pricing. We'll lay out both options and let you decide.
