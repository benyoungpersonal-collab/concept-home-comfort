Signs to watch for

Signs Your Water Heater Needs Help.

Valley water is hard, so heaters here wear out faster than the label says. Watch for these.

  • You run out of hot water faster than you used to
  • Popping, rumbling, or banging from the tank
  • Rusty or cloudy hot water
  • Water around the base of the tank
  • The tank is more than 10 years old
  • The pilot light keeps going out
  • Hot water smells like rotten eggs
  • Your tankless heater shows an error code

Tank or tankless?

Both Work. Your Home Decides.

A tank costs less and swaps in fast. Tankless lasts longer and never runs out of hot water, but it costs more up front and may need a bigger gas line.

We look at your budget, your space, and how much hot water your family uses, then tell you plainly which one we would put in our own house.
