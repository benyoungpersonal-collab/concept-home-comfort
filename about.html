Why "Pinwheel"?

Named for the windmills on the way home.

Drive east over the Altamont and the hills are covered in turning blades. For anyone who grew up in Tracy, those windmills mean you're almost home.

A pinwheel is moving air, and moving air is our whole job. Two warm blades for heating, two cool blades for cooling, all turning around one steady center. That center is the promise we make to every family we work for.

From the owner

“I still pick up the phone.”

My dad fixed swamp coolers all over Tracy in the eighties. I rode along in summer and learned that people remember how you treat them more than what you fixed.

When we started Pinwheel, Carmen and I wrote four promises on a whiteboard in our garage. We still read them at every Monday meeting. We are bigger now, with 14 trained techs and six cities, but the whiteboard hasn't changed.

If we ever miss the mark, call or text me at (209) 555-0142. I read every message.
